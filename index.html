About ILM Lab

The Intelligent Learning and Modeling (ILM) Lab is a distributed research group bringing together academic expertise from institutions across the globe, including Qassim University, Jouf University, and North South University.

The word "Ilm" (علم) translates to knowledge and science. True to our name, we are dedicated to expanding the frontiers of computational intelligence. We focus on bridging the gap between highly parameterized foundation models and resource-constrained real-world environments.

Edge AI

Deploying models on constrained IoT and clinical hardware.

Medical Imaging

Segmentation, diagnostics, and open-set anomaly rejection.

LLM Safety

Analyzing cross-lingual vulnerabilities and latent space geometries.

Explainable AI

Creating interpretable frameworks for critical decision-making.
